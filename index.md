# Fitness Agent Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 4, 2026**

Fitness Agent is a personal fitness and nutrition application designed to
analyze fitness, recovery, sleep, activity, body measurement, and nutrition
information to provide personalized fitness insights.

## Information Accessed

With the user's authorization, Fitness Agent may access data from WHOOP,
including:

- Recovery information
- Heart rate variability (HRV)
- Resting heart rate
- Sleep information
- Daily strain and cycle information
- Workout information
- Body measurements such as height, weight, and maximum heart rate

Fitness Agent may also process information voluntarily provided by the user,
such as weight measurements, meals, food photographs, nutrition information,
training schedules, and fitness goals.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to provide fitness, nutrition, recovery, and
training analysis and recommendations to the authorized user.

## Data Sharing

Fitness Agent does not sell personal information.

Personal fitness information is not shared with third parties except when
necessary to provide functionality explicitly requested by the user.

## Data Storage and Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and
personal fitness information. Authentication secrets and access tokens are
not intentionally exposed publicly.

## User Control

Users may revoke Fitness Agent's access to their WHOOP account at any time
through their WHOOP account settings.

Users may also request deletion of data stored by Fitness Agent.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or requests concerning personal data,
please contact the application owner through the contact information
associated with the Fitness Agent WHOOP application.
